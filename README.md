# Interview Signal

A desktop app for people who run job interviews: agency recruiters, in-house recruiters and hiring
managers. It captures the call on the interviewer's own computer, so no bot joins, and it works the
same on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and phone screens.

- **During the call:** a live transcript and a question guide built from the job description, with
  must-ask questions ticked off as they are covered, and suggested follow-ups.
- **After the call:** a scorecard in which every score carries a quote checked against the
  transcript, and a client submittal in the agency's own template in about a minute.
- **Privacy:** transcripts, quotes, candidate names, notes and submittals stay on the recruiter's
  computer and are never uploaded.

Windows 10 and 11, macOS 13 or newer. Free for 20 minutes with no card.
